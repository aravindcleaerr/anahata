# Anahata

Live ECG and breathing from a chest ECG board, in a phone or PC browser, over Bluetooth.

Open the app: https://aravindcleaerr.github.io/anahata/

## What it needs

- A board running the Anahata firmware, advertising as `AiiraECG`.
- A browser with Web Bluetooth: Chrome on Android, Chrome or Edge on a PC. On an iPhone, the Bluefy browser. Safari does not support it.

Without a board, press **Demo signal** to see a made-up trace, or **Open recording** to replay a saved file.

## Notes

- Recordings stay on the device. Nothing is sent to a server.
- After the first visit the app works offline, and can be installed to the home screen.

Demonstration only. Not a medical device.
